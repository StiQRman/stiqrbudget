Take control of your money without surrendering your financial privacy. StiQRbudget is a zero-knowledge, offline-first envelope budgeting and expense tracker designed for people who believe their spending habits belong strictly to them.

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE & POST-QUANTUM HYBRID ENCRYPTION
- Your financial data is encrypted directly on your device before it ever touches the network.
- Protected by Post-Quantum Hybrid Encryption (ML-KEM-768 + X25519 + AES-256-GCM) and local PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation.
- No bank logins, no account scrapers, no ads, and no tracking SDKs.

SMART ENVELOPE BUDGETING
- Organize your income into clear, customizable envelopes for bills, groceries, savings, and goals.
- Automate paycheck allocations with flexible rules: % of Check, Fixed $ Per Check, and Monthly Target funding.
- Track expenses, transfers, and balances with a fast, distraction-free interface.

PRIVATE QR COLLABORATION
- Share specific budgets with a partner or family member using end-to-end encrypted QR invites and Ed25519 signatures.
- Collaborate in real time without ever sharing bank credentials.

OFFLINE-FIRST & DATA SOVEREIGNTY
- Works seamlessly offline and syncs encrypted ciphertexts across your devices when online.
- Export your budgets, envelopes, and full transaction history to CSV at any time for free.

Your money. Your keys. 100% private.
